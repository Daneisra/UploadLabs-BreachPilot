[h1]BreachPilot – Hacking Automation & Advisor[/h1]

Keeps each Breach node on its most profitable threat level, and suggests the best wiring for your hacking chain. It plays by the game’s rules: no damage, reward or resource is changed, nothing is created for free.

[i]Unofficial mod, not affiliated with EnigmaDev Studios. Version 0.6.0. The interface follows the game’s language: English, or French for a game in French.[/i]

[h2]Auto threat regulation[/h2] (off by default)
[list]
[*]Sets each Breach node to the level that pays most per minute for your objective: XP, data, intel or balanced. Elite levels included.
[*]Climbs once to the highest unlocked level when it can be won, to open the next one, then comes back.
[*]Three profiles: conservative, balanced, aggressive.
[*]Works from the attacks it observes on your chain: unknown nodes and nodes from other mods do not get in the way.
[*]Pause and Resume on each node. Changing a level yourself pauses that node type.
[*]Safeguards: suspended at higher game speed or with the game’s own auto level option, a check after every change, a stop on any anomaly, emergency stop on [b]Ctrl+Alt+F10[/b].
[/list]

[h2]Breach Advisor[/h2] (read-only)
[list]
[*]Open it with [b]Ctrl+Alt+F8[/b] or the [b]Advice[/b] button on a Breach node.
[*]The best wiring for your objective first, step by step, with the estimated gain, drawbacks and reliability, then the recommended purchase.
[*]Things to try: nodes without power or damage, free outputs and inputs, unlocked nodes not placed yet.
[*]Detailed figures in a collapsible section. It never changes your wiring or buys anything.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe and start the game. No other mod is required: the Mod Loader comes with the game.
[*]If you installed BreachPilot by hand before, delete its zip from the game’s [b]mods[/b] folder: keep one copy only.
[*]The advisor works right away. To let the mod change threat levels, set [b]regulation_mode[/b] to [b]active[/b] in the mod’s options, for example with the [b]ModManager[/b] mod, then restart the game.
[*]Pick your objective at the top of the advisor panel. The regulation follows it.
[/olist]
Other options: [b]regulation_profile[/b] (conservative, balanced, aggressive), [b]max_allowed_level[/b] (0 means no limit), [b]min_level[/b].

[h2]Uninstalling[/h2]
Unsubscribe. Your saves stay intact: the mod writes nothing into them, and the levels it chose are ordinary game levels.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tested on Upload Labs 3.0.12 with the bundled Godot Mod Loader 7.0.1, on Windows.
[*]Tested alongside Smart GPU Manager, Smart Thread Manager, Better Splitters, Where Should I Connect?, Easy Tokens and ModManager.
[*]Extends no game script. It disables itself cleanly if a game function it needs is missing.
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]A new chain or node needs a few attacks before it gets figures.
[*]An anomaly stops the regulation until the game is restarted, on purpose.
[*]Purchases are compared one at a time.
[*]Free slots of a node type already placed (“1/3”) are not read yet.
[*]Some infection mechanics were measured on one chain only.
[*]After a language change, the game keeps some of its own texts in the former language until it restarts.
[/list]
